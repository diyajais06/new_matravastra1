## Full original brief (verbatim, authoritative)

### 1. VISUAL IDENTITY & COLOR THEME
- Primary theme: Deep Matte Black.
- Secondary colors: Antique Gold, Champagne Gold, Warm Ivory and subtle Maroon.
- Use luxurious gradients, glass effects, soft shadows and realistic 3D depth.
- Typography premium, elegant and highly readable; luxury serif headings + modern sans-serif body.
- Use the original MATRA VASTRA logo provided by the client. Do not redesign or distort the logo. (User will upload logo; until it arrives use a placeholder text logo that is easy to swap.)

### 2. CINEMATIC 3D BACKGROUND ANIMATION
Background should feel alive throughout the website. Animated Kashi-inspired environment:
- Cinematic 3D view of the ancient Varanasi Ghats.
- Subtle 3D silhouette of Kashi Vishwanath Temple architecture.
- Flowing Ganga water with realistic reflections.
- Floating golden particles and soft glowing lights.
- Animated Banarasi silk fabric moving gently in the background.
- 3D textile patterns, traditional Indian motifs and woven fabric textures.
- Subtle floating clothing graphics inspired by Kashi culture.
- Slow-moving parallax layers that react to mouse movement.
- Cinematic lighting, depth of field and smooth camera transitions.
Background continues smoothly while scrolling. Sophisticated, not flashy or distracting.

### 3. HERO SECTION
- Headline: "JAHAN SE HUM AATE HAIN."
- Subheading: "Not Just Clothing. A Legacy of Culture, Identity & Kashi."
- Large premium 3D MATRA VASTRA logo, animated golden typography, 3D clothing showcase with floating T-shirts and fabric, cinematic Kashi background.
- CTAs: "EXPLORE COLLECTION", "DISCOVER OUR STORY". Smooth entrance animations.

### 4. THREE SIGNATURE COLLECTIONS (visually different)
- COLLECTION 01 — KASHI / SPIRITUAL: Kashi typography, Mahadev-inspired artwork, Ganga & Ghats graphics, Banaras motifs. Deep black, antique gold, spiritual cinematic.
- COLLECTION 02 — APNA PURVANCHAL: Purvanchal culture, local identity & heritage, traditional patterns, modern regional typography. Rich earthy colors, heritage textures, bold graphics.
- COLLECTION 03 — ASPIRANT LIFE: study motivation graphics, aspirant lifestyle typography, minimalist educational designs, youth oversized T-shirts. Modern, stylish, contemporary.

### 5. PREMIUM 3D PRODUCT SHOWCASE
Each product: high-quality product photography (realistic mockups, no random unrelated clothing photos — AI-generated mockups OK as demo; user may give real photos later), 3D rotating T-shirt preview where possible, front and back views, name and price, sizes S/M/L/XL/XXL, color selection, Add to Cart, Buy Now, hover/reveal animations.

### 6. INTERACTIVE EXPERIENCE
Smooth scrolling, 3D hover effects, scroll-triggered animations, parallax, animated collection cards, elegant page transitions, premium navbar, animated shopping bag icon, wishlist, floating WhatsApp button, responsive mobile nav.

### 7. PAGES
Home, Shop All, Kashi Collection, Apna Purvanchal Collection, Aspirant Life Collection, Product Details, About MatraVastra, Bulk & Custom Orders, Contact Us, Shopping Cart, Checkout, Order Tracking.

### 8. BRAND STORY
Headline: "FROM THE SOUL OF KASHI."
Description: "MatraVastra brings together the timeless soul of Kashi and the language of modern fashion. Every design carries a feeling, an identity and a story."
Elegant animated storytelling with cinematic Kashi visuals.

### 9. BULK & CUSTOM ORDERS
Enquiry for colleges/institutions, ashrams/spiritual orgs, events/festivals, corporate merchandise, customized T-shirt printing. Fields: Name, Phone Number, Organization, Quantity, Customization Details, WhatsApp Enquiry Button.

### 10. PERFORMANCE
Fully responsive; optimized 3D; lazy loading; smooth on normal laptops; Three.js / React Three Fiber; GSAP or Framer Motion; graceful fallbacks for weak devices; accessibility and readable contrast; fast loading.
